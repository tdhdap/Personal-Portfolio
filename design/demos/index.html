Background animation demos

Seven directions for the portfolio's ambient background, each as its own standalone page so they can be compared side by side. Move your mouse over each one.

Add ?motion=on to any URL if it looks static (Windows "reduce motion" override).

  • 01Neural networkNodes drift and connect with thin lines when close; the cursor acts as an extra node pulling nearby links toward it. Open
  • 02Topographic contoursElevation-map contour lines from a shifting height field. The cursor drops a bump that ripples the contours outward, like a stone in water. Open
  • 03Gradient descentA loss-landscape contour map with points visibly walking downhill toward local minima, looping. A quiet nod to the actual domain. Open
  • 04Halftone breathingA dot grid whose size and opacity pulse slowly, like a print halftone. Dots near the cursor grow. Calmer than any line-based option. Open
  • 05Flowing threadNo grid at all, just two thin ribbons trailing the cursor with physics-based lag, wandering on their own when idle. Open
  • 06Wireframe terrainThe original perspective wave-terrain, rebuilt as a connected wireframe mesh instead of loose dots. More CAD, less particles.js. Open
  • 07Grain & driftMaximum restraint: fine film grain plus an almost imperceptibly slow light drift. No cursor interaction at all. Open
