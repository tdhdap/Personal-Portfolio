Predicting Backdoor Recovery Cost in Vision-Language Models

—

Investigated whether ASR-G can predict the cost of removing backdoors from large vision-language models. Using BLIP-2, the study evaluates 20 backdoored models across 120 recovery experiments and finds that recovery cost rises sharply with ASR-G, providing a basis for choosing between targeted repair and full retraining.

—

Lightweight PDE Image Denoiser

—

Developed a physics-constrained PDE model in PyTorch to filter Poisson noise from digital images using a mathematics based architecture. By replacing dense convolutions with geometric finite-differences, this lightweight approach reduces trainable parameters by 97.6% reducing training time from hours to minutes while preventing visual artifacts and maintaining competitive quality.

Satellite Imagery Risk Detection Pipeline

—

Developed a deep-learning pipeline to automate power line inspections by identifying vegetation encroachment from Sentinel-2 satellite imagery. By combining pretrained models with corridor locations and bi-temporal vegetation-change signals, the packaged tool classifies risk with high accuracy (AUC 0.79, F1 0.83) on unseen regions. The main goal is to inform power line companies, and warn them of potential harm to their powerlines caused by encroachment.
